02
Stages 1–3 · Physical separation, no reaction

Desalt and distil

First the salt and water come out. Then heat and boiling point split the crude into cuts, and vacuum recovers the heavy gas oil without cracking it.

Desalter V-101

  • Wash water + demulsifier dissolve and free the salt.
  • An electric field makes droplets coalesce and settle.
  • Brine goes to effluent treatment; two stages in series are common.
  • At OXY much of this is done upstream during stabilisation; the refinery desalter is the last line of defence.
Enters≈ 18 ptb salt, 0.1 vol% water
→
Leaves≈ 1 ptb salt, ≤ 0.1–0.3 vol% water

Atmospheric column H-101 · C-101

  • Furnace heats crude to 350–370 °C; it partly vaporises.
  • Vapour rises, liquid falls; each tray approaches equilibrium, so light ends move up.
  • Side draws take cuts out; steam strips the residue.
  • Pumparounds remove heat mid-column and feed it back to the preheat train.
EntersDesalted crude at 350–370 °C
→
LeavesGas, naphtha, kerosene, gas oil + residue

Vacuum column C-201

  • Above about 370 °C hydrocarbons crack and form coke and gums.
  • At 20–50 mmHg absolute, boiling points drop, so heavy cuts are recovered safely.
  • Ejectors hold the vacuum; packing keeps pressure drop low.

Stage 4 · Where the value is added

Conversion units

Distillation only separates what is already in the crude. Conversion units change the molecules: they crack heavy ones into lighter, more valuable ones, or reshape light ones into high-octane gasoline.

04
Stages 5–6 · Clean-up, hydrogen and utilities

Treating and sulfur recovery

Sulfur matters most. Hydrotreating turns it into H₂S, the amine unit concentrates it, and the Claus unit converts it into elemental sulfur that leaves the refinery as a product.

Hydrotreating HDT

  • Feed + H₂ over CoMo / NiMo on alumina.
  • 300–400 °C · 30–100 bar
  • R-S-R′ + 2 H₂ → RH + R′H + H₂S
  • Nitrogen leaves as NH₃; metals stay on the catalyst.

Amine sweetening MDEA

  • Lean amine absorbs H₂S from refinery gas.
  • Rich amine is heated; acid gas goes to the Claus unit.
  • MDEA beats MEA on selectivity and regeneration energy.

Claus sulfur recovery SRU

  • One third of the H₂S burns to SO₂.
  • 2 H₂S + SO₂ → 3 S + 2 H₂O
  • 95–98% recovery; a tail gas unit (SCOT) takes it higher.

Caustic and Merox

  • NaOH removes H₂S and mercaptans from LPG and light liquids.
  • Merox oxidises mercaptans to disulfides with catalyst and air.
  • Spent caustic needs neutralising before disposal.

Sour water stripper

  • Steam strips H₂S and NH₃ from process water.
  • Stripped water is reused, often as desalter wash water.

Hydrogen and utilities SMR + PSA

  • CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3 H₂, then shift: CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂.
  • PSA purifies H₂ to about 99.9 vol%.
  • Steam, cooling water, fuel gas, flare and effluent treatment serve every unit.

Stage 7 · Blend, test, release

Products and specifications

Streams from many units are blended in calculated ratios and dosed with additives. The lab releases a batch only when it meets its specification.

Gasoline
M91 · M95 · M98

Reformate, FCC gasoline, alkylate, isomerate, butane. Oman's standard aligned to Euro 5 in July 2019.

Diesel
10 mg/kg S

Hydrotreated and hydrocracked gas oil. Oman's draft spec (2020) follows EN 590; cetane ≥ 51.

Jet A-1
−47 °C freeze max

Treated kerosene. Flash point ≥ 38 °C, sulfur ≤ 0.30 wt%, specific energy ≥ 42.8 MJ/kg.

By-products
S · coke

Sulfur ≥ 99.9 wt% pure. Duqm also sells petroleum coke; its sulfur follows the crude.

Blending rules of thumb

  • Octane blends roughly by volume, but not linearly, so refiners use blending indices.
  • Sulfur blends by mass: one high-sulfur component can push the batch off spec.
  • Vapour pressure is trimmed with butane within the seasonal limit.
  • Every batch is sampled, tested and certified before release.

Limits are the commonly quoted values of each standard. Check the current edition and the buyer's contract before use.
